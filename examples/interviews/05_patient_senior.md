# Interview 05 — Pedro, 67, retired, physiotherapy patient

_Fictional interview for demo purposes. Attends physio twice a week after knee surgery._

**Interviewer:** Pedro, how do you usually book your sessions?

**Pedro:** I call. My daughter installed an app from another clinic once, but I couldn't get in. It asked for a password with capital letters and numbers, and then it sent a code to an email I don't check. I gave up. The letters are tiny too.

**Interviewer:** And how is calling for you?

**Pedro:** Calling is fine, the girls at the front desk are lovely. But sometimes it takes a long time for them to answer, and I have to call back. For me it's no problem, I'm retired, I have time. But I see they are always very busy.

**Interviewer:** Do you get reminders about your sessions?

**Pedro:** They send a message on WhatsApp and I reply "OK". That works well for me. What I don't like is when they change the time of my session and only tell me when I arrive. It happened once because my physiotherapist was sick. I took two buses to get there for nothing.

**Interviewer:** What would make things easier for you?

**Pedro:** Simple things. Big letters. If they must change something, a message or a call before I leave home. And no passwords, please.
