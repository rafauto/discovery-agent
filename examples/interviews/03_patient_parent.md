# Interview 03 — Juliana, patient and mother of two

_Fictional interview for demo purposes. Uses a dermatology clinic and the kids' dentist._

**Interviewer:** Juliana, tell me about the last time you booked an appointment.

**Juliana:** It was for my son, at the dentist. I called three times during my lunch break and the line was always busy. In the end I sent a WhatsApp and they replied the next day. By then the time I wanted was gone. I just wanted to see the free times and pick one, like booking a restaurant.

**Interviewer:** Why does it need to be at lunch?

**Juliana:** Because the clinic only answers during business hours, which are exactly my working hours. At night, when I actually have time to organise the family's agenda, there's nobody there.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever missed an appointment?

**Juliana:** Yes, I'm embarrassed to say. With two kids and work, I simply forgot. They sent a message two days before, but two days before doesn't help me — I need a reminder on the day, a few hours before. And it would be great if it went into my phone calendar automatically.

**Interviewer:** What about rescheduling?

**Juliana:** That's the worst part. If something comes up, I know I should cancel, but it means calling again, waiting, explaining. Sometimes I just give up and don't go, which I know isn't fair to the clinic. If there was a button to move it, I'd move it.

**Interviewer:** Anything else that bothers you about the process?

**Juliana:** Waiting room delays. Last time we waited 50 minutes after the scheduled time with a six-year-old. Nobody told us the doctor was running late. If they had sent a message, I'd have stayed home a bit longer.
